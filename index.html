  • Min/max trade limits — skip tiny copies and cap large ones (default $10–$500 per mirrored trade)
  • Min whale trade size — only copy whale trades above a threshold (default $1,000)
  • Copy sells — optionally mirror the whale's exit trades, not just entries
  • Per-whale enable/disable — toggle individual whales on or off
  • Per-whale mode — assign each whale to spot, perp, or both
  • Auto-disable underperformers — whales with win rate below threshold (default 30%) are auto-disabled
  • Per-whale performance tracking — trades copied, wins, losses, total P&L in USD
  • Token safety checks — only copy trades for tokens that pass safety filters
  • Liquidity requirement — minimum DEX liquidity for copied tokens (default $10,000)
  • Slippage control — max slippage in basis points for mirror swaps (default 300 bps = 3%)
  • Stop loss, take profit, and trailing stop applied to every mirrored position
  • Max simultaneous mirror positions (default 10)
  • Dry-run mode — simulate mirror trades without executing real swaps
  • Works for both Spot (Jupiter swaps) and Perpetuals (Orderly orders)
  • Default whale list included — well-known Solana smart-money addresses preloaded
  • Add/remove whales via GUI or MCP — natural language or manual entry
  • Persistent whale list, trade log, and performance tracking
  • Configurable poll interval — how often to check whale wallets (default 10s)
